| OQ-21 | Cash and positions as separately processable chains | "I dont want cash calculated on the go. It should be precalculated. How could cash and position/trades be split in a way I can impact the cash if I want with a processing or only impact trades or both together."; then, after the design: "looks good. Communicate the lanes" | 1. Two independent daily chains read the same Ledger facts and never each other's output. The **cash chain**, `cash[D] = cash[D−1] − Σ(signed_qty × execution_PU) + deposits − withdrawals`, reads trades and cash movements from Ledger and needs no positions and no prices. The **positions chain** computes quantities and average PU/rate, then valuation (`financial_total`, market value) and P&L with reference prices. They meet only in AUM = cash + market value. 2. Dirty markers per chain: `accounting_dirty_from` is split into `cash_dirty_from` and `positions_dirty_from`; `valuation_dirty_from` stays. A trade created or deleted on D makes cash **and** positions stale from D; a deposit or withdrawal makes only cash stale; a reference price makes only valuation stale. 3. Revisions per input: `ledger_revision` is split into `trades_revision` and `cash_movements_revision`. A CASH job fences on trades and cash movements; a POSITIONS job on trades and prices; so a deposit does not supersede a running positions job, and a new trade supersedes both. 4. `processing.requests.scope` ∈ {CASH, POSITIONS, ALL}, required. Each scope checks its own A−1 predecessor and raises its own `STALE_PREDECESSOR`, fences its own revisions and advances only its own markers to B+1 (OQ-12); ALL does both in one atomic publish. Still exactly [A, B] (OQ-11), one active job, all-or-nothing publish (§10.4). 5. Storage: a new published table `portfolio.cash_balances(balance_date, cash_balance, trades_cash_flow, deposits, withdrawals, updated_at)` and its staging twin, owned by Portfolio (OQ-8); `portfolio_metrics.cash_balance` is dropped. The AUM CHECK cannot span two tables, so it becomes a publish invariant: whichever scope publishes recomputes `aum` for its days from the other chain's published value, in the same transaction (one job runs at a time). AUM is `null` when either side is missing and reads stale when either side is stale; an integration test checks `aum = cash + market value`. 6. The invalidator replaces `ledger_changed` with `trades_changed(effective_date, *, uow)` and `cash_movements_changed(effective_date, *, uow)`; `prices_changed` stays. `ProcessingRequest` gains `scope`. 7. UI: the processing form and Processamentos offer the scope (**Caixa**, **Posições**, **Tudo**); the portfolio summary reports cash freshness separately from positions and valuation; the Lançamentos strip reads the published cash from `cash_balances` with its own stale flag (amends OQ-19, item 3). |
