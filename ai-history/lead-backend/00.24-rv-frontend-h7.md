When a job finishes, the results now refresh on every screen, not only on Central de Controle. I checked it against the real API on Lançamentos, but on Carteira only in the mock tests.

**Fix:**
- The job polling and the refresh when a job ends moved out of Central de Controle into a new `useJobWatcher` in [useProcessing.ts](frontend/src/features/processing/hooks/useProcessing.ts). It polls only while a job is queued or running.
- A `JobWatcher` component that renders nothing is mounted once next to `AppLayout` in [routes.tsx](frontend/src/app/routes.tsx). It can't go inside `AppLayout` because `shared/` may not import a feature.
- Central de Controle's `useProcessingRequests` reads the same cached job list without polling, so there is only one poll per 2 s even when it is open.

**Tests:** I added [JobWatcher.test.tsx](frontend/src/features/processing/JobWatcher.test.tsx), written before the fix:
- Carteira refreshes when a job ends on another screen.
- The Lançamentos cash strip refreshes both dates when a job ends.
- No polling from another screen when no job is active.
- Exactly one poll per 2 s with Central de Controle open.

The first three failed before the fix, which reproduces H7. The fourth guards against double polling and passed before and after.
